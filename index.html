Frequently asked questions

Questions before you apply?

Is DBA suitable for beginners?+

Yes. The program begins with business and research foundations, then progresses step by step through building, launching, selling, automation and scaling.

Can I attend DBA online?+

Yes. DBA is designed for both physical classroom delivery and live online classes through Zoom, supported by LMS resources.

How long is the program?+

DBA runs for six months with two scheduled classes per week.

Will students work on practical projects?+

Yes. Students complete a business blueprint, working store, launch system, acquisition portfolio, AI workflow and final capstone.

Does the program include Shopify and WordPress?+

Yes. Students receive practical exposure to Shopify and WordPress/WooCommerce, including AI-assisted store-building workflows.

Will I receive a certificate?+

A completion certificate is awarded according to Enablers’ final attendance, project and program-completion requirements.
